Dhun is an independent, open-source Android music player maintained by @polikeybhuvan.

<b>Features:</b>

* Search for songs and artists
* Stream music with background playback
* Save tracks for offline listening
* Create and manage playlists
* Browse recently played songs
* View artist pages and lyrics when available
* Light and dark themes
* Playback speed controls, sleep timer, and queue management

Dhun is an independent fork of ViMusic. It retains the original GPL-3.0 license and attribution. YouTube and related marks belong to their respective owners; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
